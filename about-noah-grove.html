Noah Grove: singer-songwriter, traveler, performer, and builder of creative systems

Noah Grove is a Charleston-based singer-songwriter, performer, producer, kitesurfer, basketball player, philosopher, and thinker whose music life has moved through studios, open mics, tours, vans, beaches, cities, and live-looping performance systems.

Who is Noah Grove?

Noah Grove is a singer-songwriter and live performer based in Charleston, South Carolina. His work connects original songs, acoustic performance, live looping, DJ sets, recording, community open mics, creative technology, and a lifelong interest in meaning, travel, and human experience.

He has self-produced four albums and has played music in a wide range of places while living in Miami, the Outer Banks, Los Angeles, Nashville, and Charleston.

A life built around music and motion

Noah has traveled to Canada, Mexico, England, Ireland, India, China, Morocco, Argentina, Turkey, France, Germany, and Austria. He lived in his car in Los Angeles while chasing his music dream, taught kitesurfing in the Outer Banks, and later drove a graffiti-covered van around the United States and into Mexico on the 2016 Big Picture tour.

He earned a BA in Psychology from the University of Tennessee, Knoxville, and his interest in psychology, philosophy, music, and experience continues to shape the way he writes, performs, and builds creative systems.

Big Picture album release show at Zafaria Station in Long Beach, California.

Charleston chapter

Noah and his wife have lived in Charleston since 2018. In Charleston, Noah has performed live music, hosted and organized open mic communities, built The Grove studio identity, ran the internet open mic eOM during COVID, developed Mic Club, and formed the band Noah and The Space Ark before evolving the project into a one-man-band loop station and DJ console.
