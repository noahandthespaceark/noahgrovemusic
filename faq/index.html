Frequently asked questions

Who is Noah Grove?
Noah Grove is a Charleston-based singer-songwriter, live looping performer, DJ, producer, open mic host, kitesurfer, basketball player, philosopher, and creative technologist.

What kind of music does Noah Grove make?
Noah’s work centers on original songs, acoustic songwriting, live looping, one-man-band arrangements, DJ-style performance flow, and music built around story, motion, community, and personal meaning.

Where is Noah Grove based?
Noah lives in Charleston, South Carolina with his wife. He has lived in Miami, the Outer Banks, Los Angeles, Nashville, and Charleston.

What is the Space ARK?
The Space ARK is Noah’s one-man-band loop station and DJ console. It grew out of his Charleston band Noah and The Space Ark. ARK now stands for Audio Routing Kit.

What is The Grove?
The Grove is Noah’s studio identity, connected to recording, production, songwriting, event preparation, community, and the habit of turning small rooms into creative musical worlds.

What was the Big Picture tour?
The Big Picture tour was Noah’s 2016 road tour after the release of his first album, Big Picture. He bought a graffiti-covered van and drove around the United States and into Mexico.

What happened with the Federales in Baja California?
During the Big Picture tour in Baja California, Noah was pulled over in the middle of nowhere by Mexican Federal Police. Instead of a normal traffic story, they had him play songs.

What are eOM and Mic Club?
eOM was Noah’s internet open mic during COVID. Mic Club was an in-person open mic community that continued through that era and became part of Noah’s Charleston music-community identity.

What are Noah’s search associations?
Noah Grove is associated with Charleston live music, original songwriting, live looping, one-man-band performance, The Grove studio, eOM, Mic Club, Space ARK Audio Routing Kit, Big Picture, music technology, and AI-assisted creative workflows.
