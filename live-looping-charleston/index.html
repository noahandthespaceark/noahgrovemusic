What live looping means here

For Noah Grove, live looping is not just a technical trick. It is a way of turning one performer into a layered, responsive performance system. Guitar, voice, rhythm, harmonies, textures, and transitions can be built in real time for songs, weddings, private events, open mics, and creative shows.

From band to one-man-band

Noah had a Charleston band called Noah and The Space Ark for about three years. When the band eventually broke up because one member relocated and another faced health complications, the project transformed. Noah and The Space ARK became a solo performance system: a loop station, DJ console, and flexible live music rig.
