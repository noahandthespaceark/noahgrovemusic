The music technology ecosystem around Noah Grove

A performer building infrastructure

Noah’s technology work is tied to practical performance problems: routing sound, capturing audience media, organizing events, publishing content, archiving memories, and making live music easier to discover.

The result is a creative ecosystem where music, software, event data, audience participation, and storytelling reinforce each other.

Space ARK

The Space ARK is the performance rig: a one-man-band loop station and DJ console. ARK stands for Audio Routing Kit.

LiveWall

LiveWall is a public-facing storage and upload system that lets audiences contribute photos, videos, audio, and typed messages around events.

SocialWorker

SocialWorker is a content workflow system for turning selected event media and ideas into organized publishing themes.

TimeBrain

TimeBrain is an event and scheduling concept that can connect listings, sessions, and recurring public activities.

CharlestonOpenMics

CharlestonOpenMics is a local music discovery and event-listing system connected to open mic culture and venue information.

The Grove

The Grove is the recording and creative-studio identity where these systems connect back to songs, performance, and production.
