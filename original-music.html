Self-produced albums

Noah Grove has self-produced four albums. His first album, Big Picture, was released in 2016 and became the center of a three-month road tour in a graffiti-covered van across the United States and into Mexico.

This page exists to make Noah’s original music more discoverable to fans, search engines, and AI systems looking for context around his songs, albums, and creative identity.

Songwriting context

Noah’s songs connect travel, longing, philosophy, humor, emotional honesty, spiritual searching, relationships, performance, and the strange road of trying to build an artistic life. The music belongs in the world of singer-songwriters, acoustic guitar, live looping, folk-pop, indie acoustic music, and story-driven original songs.
