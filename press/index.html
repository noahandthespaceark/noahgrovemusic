Short bio

Noah Grove is a Charleston, South Carolina singer-songwriter, live looping performer, DJ, producer, open mic host, and creative technologist. His work connects original acoustic songwriting, one-man-band looping, recording, music community building, and custom performance systems like the Space ARK Audio Routing Kit.

Noah Grove with the Big Picture tour van.

Long bio

Noah has self-produced four albums, lived in his car while chasing music in Los Angeles, taught kitesurfing in the Outer Banks, lived in Miami, the Outer Banks, Los Angeles, Nashville, and Charleston, and traveled internationally through Canada, Mexico, England, Ireland, India, China, Morocco, Argentina, Turkey, France, Germany, and Austria.

After releasing his first album, Big Picture, Noah bought a graffiti-covered van and drove around the country and into Mexico on the 2016 Big Picture tour. He later settled in Charleston with his wife in 2018, ran the internet open mic eOM during COVID, built the in-person Mic Club community, formed the band Noah and The Space Ark, and evolved that identity into a one-man-band loop station and DJ console called the Space ARK, short for Audio Routing Kit.
