A studio identity

Noah Grove has a studio called The Grove. The name connects his recording work, production, songwriting sessions, event preparation, experiments, and creative community.

Earlier in his music life, Noah ran a recording studio in a detached old carriage house in Long Beach, California. The space was given to him by supporters of the arts, Richard and Therese.

Nathan Douglas recordings

Songwriter Nathan Douglas hired Noah to record and produce a few hundred of his songs over the course of years. The project involved around six dozen different artists and musicians, making The Grove part of a large collaborative songwriting and production history.
